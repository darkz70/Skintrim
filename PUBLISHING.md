
# Skintrim

**Change your skin and cape without ever leaving the game.**

Press **K**, drop in a PNG — from your disk, from a link, or just type any
player's username — and your new look is on. Skintrim remembers everything
you wore in a built-in **history**, so your favorite outfits are always one
click away.

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **Skins from anywhere** — a local PNG file, an `https://` image URL, or any Minecraft username (fetched straight from Mojang profiles)
- 🖱️ **Drag & drop** — throw a PNG at the game window and it applies itself (files named `*cape*` apply as capes)
- 🧍 **Classic & Slim** models, with a live 2D preview in the editor
- 📜 **History** — your last 16 skins and 16 capes, one click to re-wear; entries survive deleted files and dead links thanks to cached copies
- 💾 Everything is **saved automatically** and restored on your next launch
- 🌐 In-game config via hotkey (**K**) or ModMenu

## 🦸 Capes that don't need Mojang

Custom capes in Skintrim are **completely independent of Mojang**:

- No official Mojang cape required — bring any 64x32 cape PNG
- Switch cape source anytime: **Custom**, **Mojang** (your official cape), or **Hidden**
- Works in singleplayer and offline mode; your cape texture doubles as your elytra texture

## 👀 Who sees your new skin?

| Audience | How |
|---|---|
| **You** | Instantly, on any server, no setup |
| **Other Skintrim players** | Automatic sync when the server also runs Skintrim — skins *and* capes |
| **Literally everyone** (even vanilla) | One-click **Upload to Mojang** puts the skin on your official profile (licensed account, online mode). Skins only — Mojang does not accept custom capes |

## 📦 Installation

1. Install **Fabric Loader** (≥ 0.19.3) for Minecraft **26.2** or **26.3**
2. Drop in **Fabric API** and the Skintrim jar for your game version
3. Launch the game and press **K**

Server owners: put the same jar on your Fabric server to enable skin/cape
sync for all Skintrim users. The mod is client-focused and loads no client
code on a dedicated server.

## ❓ FAQ

**Does it work on vanilla servers?**
Yes — you always see your own skin/cape. For *others* to see it on a
vanilla server, use Upload to Mojang (skin only) or play on a server with
Skintrim installed (skins and capes).

**Can other players see my custom cape?**
Yes, if they use Skintrim and the server runs Skintrim. Mojang profiles
cannot store custom capes, so no mod can show a custom cape to vanilla
clients — anyone claiming otherwise is selling you a screenshot.

**Is my account token safe?**
Uploads use your current session token to talk to Mojang's official API.
The token is never logged, stored, or sent anywhere else.

**Offline / cracked launchers?**
Local skins, custom capes, history and server sync all work offline.
Only the Mojang upload needs a licensed account in online mode.
